# App icons

`XBot.ico` (Windows: 16/24/32/48/64/256) and `XBot.icns` (macOS: 16 to 1024 with @2x) are generated from the XBot logo sources (xbot-logo/final/src) and copied here as they are. The 16, 24 and 32 pixel images are tuned pixel by pixel: do not regenerate them by scaling a larger image. `scripts/package-desktop.sh` copies these files unchanged.
