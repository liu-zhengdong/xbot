Source: user-provided xbot-design-assets/avatars/modular (2026-11-09).
Original canvas: 1024 x 1024; packaged PNG layers: 512 x 512.
Prepared with scripts/avatar/prepare.py using Pillow.

base/bald.png comes from base/baseplate-bald.png.
base/head.png is a skin matte from that same image. It restores the head above
clothing and chest accessories without regenerating the face.
The remaining layers come from parts/{hair,tops,face,accessories,props}.
Transparent none/empty files are represented by missing layers.

The existing body/skin variants are opaque composites and are not packaged.
The renderer uses one body base. No interactive part editor is included.
